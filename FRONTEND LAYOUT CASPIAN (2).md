**FRONTEND LAYOUT FOR STUDENT RECRUITER PORTFOLIO**

**MAIN PAGE 🙂:**

This will be the main landing page \- displaying basic info of what our product/application does, highlighting its features.

Along with that there will be 4 buttons on the Top:

1. STUDENT REGISTER  
2. STUDENT LOGIN  
3. RECRUITER REGISTER  
4. RECRUITER LOGIN

Registration will be done for the new users only and it will redirect the people to login after registration is done.

***SEARCH AND FEED PANEL ON THE TOP***  
Here the students can search and look for the projects of other students also. But they can just look at the project’s name and summary only \- to know the other details \- a login pop up should show up.

**//**   
**NEW TAB (Different to personal analytics)**: Also, after the login \- a student cannot see the entire analytics of the other students’ project \- like he cannot see the commit history, rating changes and all that. Only details of the project, its author, recruiter interests and project metrics must be visible.  
// 

**STUDENT PAGE 🙂:**  
This will show up when the student login is successful.

Here there will be 3 tabs:

TAB 1: PERSONAL ANALYTICS  
Here, there will be a detailed analysis of every project that the student has made and uploaded including the \- summary, AI rating, commit/ change history, rating changes, recruiter suggestions, etc.

TAB 2: SEARCH AND FEED  
Look for other student’s projects. (redirected to the same search and feed tab that a person could use from the main page)

\~ Here, a community suggestion thread will operate where the other students can add their possible feedback that they want to send. Also, this message will not go to the user personally, they can just see it on their portal. 

TAB 3: ADD NEW PROJECT  
Here the student can insert new projects and its details to upload.

**RECRUITER PAGE 🙂:**    
   
This will show up when the recruiter login is successful.

Here there will be 3 tabs:

TAB 1: Personal Recommendations  
Personal recommendation is taken during registration which will remain as recommendation by defaault but you could later change it as stated below.
Here there will be a filter where the recruiter can select the categories and choices of the kind of projects that he wants to discover and only those filtered ones will show up here.

On tapping a project that he wants to know about \- he will be redirected to the personal analytics of that project.   
Here there will also be a suggestion box beneath every project’s analysis where the recruiter can write and send some message. The same message will be updated on the TAB 2 \- suggestion history and replies.

The projects that the recruiter likes can also be added to cart which gets updated on the TAB 3 \- containing the list of the wishlisted projects.

TAB 2: SUGGESTION HISTORY AND REPLIES  
Recruiters can see their suggestion history and what replies and updates by the students have come to their suggestions.

TAB 3: CART  
List of all the “added to cart” projects which can be handily accessed from here.
